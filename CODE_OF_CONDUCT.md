# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Recon Core is an open-source project for data practitioners. The community should be respectful, inclusive, and focused on building useful software.

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Expected behavior includes:

- being respectful and constructive,
- welcoming different levels of experience,
- giving and receiving feedback professionally,
- focusing criticism on ideas and code,
- assuming good intent while still addressing issues clearly,
- helping keep discussions useful and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or discrimination,
- personal attacks,
- insults or demeaning comments,
- sexualized language or imagery,
- trolling or sustained disruption,
- publishing private information without permission,
- abusive behavior in issues, pull requests, discussions, or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers through the contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md` or the repository owner profile.

Reports should include relevant links, screenshots, or context when possible.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, community channels, and events related to the project.
